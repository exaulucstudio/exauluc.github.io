Synopsis

After the mysterious death of Kazuo Hoshino behind the wheel of the GD808 VX-T prototype, Aiko Hoshino, a brilliant but tormented engineer, infiltrates the forbidden Gensei Hills circuit to uncover the truth. Competing against an experimental vehicle whose behaviour appears to adapt and against a silent corporate machine, she plunges into a paranoid race where mastery of the car may demand the ultimate sacrifice.

Logline: An engineer obsessed with the truth takes the wheel of the legendary GD808 VX-T to find out why it killed her brother, and how far she’ll go to tame it.
